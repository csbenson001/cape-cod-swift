Which viral feature categories matter most to you?

Select up to 3 that feel most aligned with your vision. These are ranked by viral potential based on research into AllTrails, Roadtrippers, and travel app trends.

A

Shareable Trip Artifacts

The #1 viral driver. Auto-generated "My Cape Cod Week" summary cards, photo stamps with location/date overlays, and bingo completion graphics designed for Instagram/TikTok. AllTrails grew to 65M users largely through shareable content loops.

Why it works

  • Every share = free marketing
  • FOMO drives downloads
  • Families already take photos — this adds the app brand
B

Live Beach Status Feed

Solves Cape Cod's #1 pain point. Crowdsourced or estimated parking availability, crowd level, and real-time conditions per beach. "Is Nauset full yet?" is the question every Cape visitor asks every morning. No app answers it.

Why it works

  • Daily utility = daily opens
  • Solves a real, urgent problem
  • Creates word-of-mouth ("this app told me Skaket had parking")
C

Cape Cod Digital Passport

Gamification that drives exploration. Check in at locations across the Cape to earn badges ("Outer Cape Explorer", "Seafood Trail", "All 10 Lighthouses"). Builds on your existing Bingo + Achievements. Dynamic badges that unlock content, not just static visuals, drive 70% higher engagement.

Why it works

  • Families compete together
  • Multi-year retention ("we got 60% last year, let's finish")
  • Badges are shareable social proof
D

Tide-Smart Day Planner

The "aha moment" feature. Auto-schedules your day around tide tables so you never miss Brewster Flats, arrive at the wrong tide, or waste time. "Visit Skaket at 2pm when the flats are out, then sunset at Rock Harbor at 7:30." No other app does this.

Why it works

  • Unique — no competitor offers this
  • Prevents the #1 tourist mistake (wrong tide)
  • Creates shareable itineraries
E

Smart Packing & Trip Prep

Pre-trip engagement. Weather-based packing lists customized for your trip dates, kids' ages, and planned activities. "Pack reef shoes for tide pooling Tuesday" and "Bring layers — Wednesday drops to 58." Engages users before they even arrive.

Why it works

  • Hooks users days before the trip
  • Moms share this with each other
  • Builds trust before arrival
F

Hidden Gems / Local Secrets

The "insider knowledge" hook. Locally-curated, off-the-beaten-path spots that aren't on TripAdvisor. "The beach locals go to," "the ice cream shop with no line," "the sunset spot nobody knows." Unlocked progressively as you explore.

Why it works

  • 30-year visitors AND newbies both want this
  • Creates exclusivity and word-of-mouth
  • Progressive unlock = retention
